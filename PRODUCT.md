# Woodland Tumble

Two players share a keyboard and fight on a tilting plank balanced over a rune sphere. Landing, defense and launch direction matter because the ground moves beneath both fighters.

Preserve the woodland pixel-art identity, native editable scene and separate Fern/Ember character assets. This rework expands combat, not the course's older virtual-pet project. The target editor is Unity 6000.5.10f1.
