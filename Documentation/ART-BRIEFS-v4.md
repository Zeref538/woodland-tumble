# Elemental slash artwork brief

Create a transparent pixel-art effects sheet with sixteen isolated animation cells. Eight frames form a mint-green woodland slash with a bright tapered white core, feathered leaf trails and scattered leaf sparks. Eight frames form a warm amber fire slash with a white-hot core and small fading cinders. Show anticipation, full sweep and graceful dissipation. Keep each frame separate with clear transparent gaps, consistent scale and no text, borders or background.

The retained sheet is Assets/Art/Sources/ElementalSlashes-source.png. Extraction preserves whole silhouettes and applies one common scale to padded 256x256 frames. VFX-FRAMES-v4.json records the slicing measurements. Eight frames per element animate through the existing pooled effects system, mirrored for left-facing attacks.
