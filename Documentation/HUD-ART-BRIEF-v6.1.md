# Tilt Rivals HUD artwork

Four round medallions on transparent backgrounds: an amber punching fist, a green kicking boot, a teal blocking shield and a violet magic-star special. Match the woodland pixel-art characters with visible square clusters, stepped outlines, a limited palette and simple dark rings with gold highlights. No text or controller glyphs are baked into the art. The initial painted draft was replaced with the pixel-art revision.

The VS mark uses bold angular pixel letters in pale mint and ivory-gold, a dark outline and a small diagonal elemental slash. It has a transparent background and remains readable at the displayed 94x52 size. No other lettering is included.

The medallion atlas is cut at its four cells, cropped to visible alpha and scaled uniformly with nearest-neighbor sampling into 256x256 canvases. The VS artwork is cropped and resized uniformly. HUD-ART-v6.1.json preserves final dimensions and hashes. Native Unity UI supplies the diamond connector and input badges separately from the artwork.
