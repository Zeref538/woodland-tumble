# Presentation pass

Keep the woodland arenas and combat rules. Replace the large title and control boxes with compact bottom fighter plates, portrait cutouts, health and shield strips, and a small top stage label. Use CC0 action recordings where suitable, with a license file and source URL. Mix jump, landing, dash, swing, hit, ward, parry and knockout at different levels and small pitch variations. Mute and volume must work while paused; pausing must stop action audio. Test audio references, emitted playback, volume/mute and the existing combat suite.
