# Tilt Rivals combat v7

Make close fighting respond promptly through shorter grounded startup and recovery, reliable input buffering and clear hit confirmation. Preserve readable counterplay, guard and stamina costs. The comparison is the feel of a fighting game; keep the original woodland characters and side-view balance rules.

Final controller layout: Square/X punches, Circle/B kicks, Cross/A jumps and double-jumps, Triangle/Y casts the ranged special. L1/LB dashes backward and R1/RB dashes forward. L2/LT alone guards. Holding both triggers requests the low-health ultimate and takes priority over guard. R2/RT alone does nothing. Keyboard retains movement, guard and dash keys; T/I requests the ultimate.

Every character gains newly drawn Dash, Backstep, Victory and Ultimate sequences. Triangle attacks reach beyond melee and have six-second cooldowns. Ultimates require at most25 health and can be used once per round; each has a visible telegraph, its own animation and a character-specific confirmed-hit effect. Misses must not trap the opponent in an unavoidable cinematic hit. End, cancellation, reset and pause must restore camera and control state.

The four illustrated action circles grow to 80 screen pixels at 1280x720 and move to the bottom left and right corners. Floating circular portraits and mirrored health/stamina meters occupy the top corners without a filled fighter-panel background. Prompts follow the assigned device. A low-health pulse signals ultimate readiness. Accepted actions animate their own icon and ring; refused clicks flash red without spending stamina or silently buffering attacks. VFX defaults to the highest existing preset while retaining manual settings controls.

Arena PVP uses newly illustrated bases and upper ledges for all four map themes, with a longer physical main platform and wider ledges. Balance retains its existing plank and sphere. The saved scene, source archive and separate artwork pack must include the new assets.
