# Menu artwork briefs

Two new scenery assets provide the title and stage-selection backgrounds. Both are saved in Assets/Art/Menu. The menus use native Unity text and buttons over the pictures; no generated text is baked into the images.

TitleBackdrop.png: a wide original pixel-art panorama of a moonlit ancient forest overlooking a luminous lake, with mossy arches, lanterns and distant towers. The right half features a moss-covered balancing plank on an enormous round rune stone. Keep the left half quiet and dark for menu text. Use deep navy, teal, warm lantern light and restrained mint/amber accents. Fill the canvas without text, characters, logos, buttons, HUD or borders.

WorldAtlas.png: a wide, three-quarter overhead pixel-art woodland island surrounded by a navy night sky. Connect three distinct regions with winding paths: a sunlit emerald forest on the left, a cobalt moonlit lake with ruined arches in the center, and an amber autumn grove on the right. Each region includes a small balancing arena landmark. Keep the whole island visible and leave a quiet lower margin for interactive UI. No labels, lettering, characters, buttons or borders.

The title art is composed for text on the left. The atlas is scenery for the map screen; native selection cards use the actual in-game arena backgrounds. Aspect-ratio fitting fills the viewport without stretching the artwork. Original output files are retained unchanged in the game and separate artwork repositories.

SkywardRuins.png: a wide opaque pixel-art panorama of floating ancient stone ruins, crystal gardens, lavender clouds and distant waterfalls. Keep the combat area open and quieter than the edges. No characters, interface or text. Saved in Assets/Resources/Art.

SkywardPlank.png and CrystalSphere.png: two separate transparent pixel-art props. A pale stone beam has a flat walkable upper surface, cyan crystal inlay and small lilac flowers. The round moonstone sphere has turquoise runes and crystal details. The original sheet remains in Assets/Art/Sources; props were mechanically cropped and scaled uniformly into matching arena canvases.

Roster briefs: ten distinct frames per action, woodland chibi proportions and fixed foot alignment. Fern and Ember gain dedicated kicks and elemental specials. Luna is a white-haired blue moon mage; Bram is a chestnut-haired gauntlet fighter. Both gain their own walk, jump, hurt, knockout, punch, kick, block and special sequences. The unchanged source sheets and exact extraction records are retained in ROSTER-ART-v6.json.
