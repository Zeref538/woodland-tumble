# Shared art direction

The approved combination is Arcade Aura interface plus Ink & Motion effects. DESIGN.md governs the result; the two reference boards are in Documentation/Design-Samples.

Use midnight purple with vivid cyan, coral pink, amber and acid lime. Clear rounded comic display lettering, calm readable label fields and confident illustrated bands define the interface. Attack effects use broad directional ink-brush sweeps, pale contact cores, teal or the fighter's own element color, and short controlled paint fragments. Keep the detailed existing fighters and preserve their proportions. Avoid wood ornaments, crystal shards, rune glyph decoration and glossy mobile-game bevels.

Every generation prompt must include these shared colors, shape rules, reference roles and exclusions, followed by the specific asset composition. Generate independent assets together. Keep source artwork, live labels and state feedback in separate layers. Inspect the actual Unity result, not just the generated images.
