# Combat visual direction

Keep the woodland art and distinct scout silhouettes. Use mint leaf slashes for Fern and amber flame slashes for Ember, with thin white cores and tapered trailing fragments. The Dimraeth combat reference informs impact clarity and local light, while these assets and side-view balance mechanics remain original.

Motion follows anticipation, contact and decay. Confirmed hits add brief impact pauses, eased rings and damped directional camera impulses. Glow stays behind fighters; bloom remains restrained so faces and silhouettes read clearly. Ambient motes change with the arena.

Compact deep-forest fighter plates sit at the lower corners. Cropped portraits identify each player. Large health numbers, delayed damage trails, ward strips and round pips stay readable without covering the arena. The pixel wordmark is small; body labels use a clearer font. F1 opens controls and the footer shows audio feedback.

Fern uses mint #61FFAD; Ember uses amber #FF7A2E. Warm white marks impact cores, red-orange marks broken guard, and dark forest grounds the interface. Each arena changes background, plank and sphere together without changing physical dimensions.

Each scout has independent native clips, a common foot pivot and aligned frame canvases. Effects share a bounded 320-sprite pool; sounds share eight voices. Pausing freezes action animation and audio. Mute and volume remain usable while paused.
