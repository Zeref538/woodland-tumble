# Approved direction: arcade interface, ink effects

Current midterm direction: INKCLASH. Use heavyweight black/navy ink plates, warm ivory brush lettering and sparse cyan/vermilion accents. Generate every static button label, backdrop, slogan and move-card header. Long menu and pause controls share a generated ink bar; stage-navigation controls use the short plate. The slogan is FIGHT SMART. FALL LAST. Remove bottom navigation hints. Move cards show the selected fighter and four dedicated action illustrations. Defense displays a real six-frame elemental shield rather than decorative sparkles. The ultimate-ready rail has a moving ink highlight.

Use Documentation/Design-Samples/Arcade-Aura.png for the main menu, HUD, lettering, bright color accents and fighter-card composition. Use Documentation/Design-Samples/Ink-and-Motion.png for sweeping brush attack arcs, contact splashes and their decay. These are approved concept boards, not implemented screenshots.

Keep the eight existing large detailed fighter designs and native animation catalogs. Do not replace them with the older four small scouts. Preserve the current five-hit knockdowns, 99-second sudden death, fixed grounded arenas and open edge manholes. No wood-frame, shard or rune decorative system. The game name remains Rune Rumble.

Build separate background, atmosphere, fixed floor, hazard, fighter, VFX and live-interface layers. Keep labels and meters editable and test their actual bounds. Generate independent assets concurrently and include the shared direction in every prompt. Check title, roster, stage selection and HUD at 1280x720, 1440x900 and 1920x800 before calling the design implemented.

Selection update: the active fighter stays on the left for both players, with four distinct high-resolution pixel-art breathing poses. The roster sits to the right. HUD circles are dedicated close-up face artwork. The map picker uses six tall slanted illustration panels and a subdued navy exposure; scenery brightness stays below fighter and effect brightness. Replace Corin's crystal identity with Marin, a navy-haired tide fighter with a teal headband and sea-blue coat.

Readability overrides the previous lettering choice: use normal upright sans-serif text, bold headings and strong white labels. Menu actions use broad black/navy ink brush silhouettes, with generous safe text insets, colored focus strokes and a small animated focus expansion. Keep text ivory on dark ink in every state. HUD action discs use purpose-drawn illustrated ability icons showing a guarding arm, fist, boot or elemental special, never crops of combat effect frames. Arena selection has a quiet dedicated background and selection lift, image zoom and a brief cyan glint.

Combat framing: use a closer camera for large fighters, preserving the physical body and sprite scale. Pull back to contain both players when they separate or jump. Hold the view during pause. Track the falling rival sideways during ring-out without following below the floor, and clamp camera travel to the background edges. The primary stage is Lantern Crossroads, a wet street-level plaza with elemental shop lights and deep perspective.

Current HUD revision: restore the controller diamond with Special above, Punch left, Kick right and Guard below. Keep centered icons and separate readable captions. Use the new VS artwork with a compact live timer underneath. Place standing fighter feet near the lower quarter of the screen, showing less paving and more arena architecture while keeping camera bounds inside the scenery. Typography uses three original upright vector typefaces: Rumble Display for headers, Rumble Signal for subheaders and numbers, and Rumble Read for body text.

Menu refinement: generate the complete static button or arena-name plaque with its lettering already contained inside the painted ink. Keep live text for changing settings, controls and descriptions. Remove full-width footer shading. The eight-rival battle backdrop leads the main menu; its darkened version supports the roster and arena picker. Cinder Quay replaces the bright primary map with dry paving, muted brick workshops and restrained blue-hour lamp light.
